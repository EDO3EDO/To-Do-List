    Common Questions

    View All
    How do I add a new task? expand_more
  • Navigate to your active Space dashboard.
  • Click on the Add Task input field or button.
  • Type your task description and set a deadline.
  • Press enter or click Save to publish the task.
    • How do I change the app language? expand_more
  • Open your Account or Profile Settings menu.
  • Look for the Language or Preferences section.
  • Select your preferred language from the dropdown list.
  • Click Save or Apply to update the interface instantly.
    • Can I sync data across multiple devices? expand_more
  • Sign in with the same account on all your devices.
  • Ensure you have an active cloud backend connection.
  • Your tasks and updates will synchronize automatically in real-time.
  • Access your updated workspace from any phone, tablet, or PC.
    • How do I delete my account permanently? expand_more
  • Go to Privacy & Security settings in your profile.
  • Scroll down to the Danger Zone or Account Management section.
  • Click on Delete Account and confirm your identity/password.
  • Note that this action is irreversible and all your spaces will be erased.
